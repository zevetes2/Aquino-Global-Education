    Declaración institucional. Aquino Global Education es una plataforma educativa que facilita el acceso a programas académicos de universidades, seminarios e institutos acreditados con los que mantiene alianzas. Aquino Global Education no es una institución acreditada ni confiere títulos por sí misma. La acreditación, la admisión final, el control académico, los títulos, las transcripciones oficiales y todo el registro académico del estudiante provienen de la institución aliada que ofrece el programa.Institutional statement. Aquino Global Education is an educational platform that facilitates access to academic programs from the accredited universities, seminaries and institutes with which it holds partnerships. Aquino Global Education is not an accredited institution and does not confer degrees on its own. Accreditation, final admission, academic oversight, degrees, official transcripts and the student's entire academic record come from the partner institution offering the program.
